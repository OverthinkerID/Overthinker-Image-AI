# Changelog — OIA – Overthinker Image AI

All notable changes to **OIA – Overthinker Image AI** are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-19
### Added
- **Phases 1–6**: Project & Immutable Logo Vault (SHA-256), Brand DNA Analyzer, 4 Brand Concepts, Fabric.js Design Engine (SVG/PNG/PDF), Smart Mockup Studio & Brand Guidelines/Presentations, Automation Engine & 14-Folder Commercial Export.
- **Phase 7**: Multi-Workspace SaaS & Local Edition (`LOCAL` / `SAAS`), RBAC, Client CRM & Portal, Collaboration Comments & Notifications, Usage & Billing Quotas, Local/S3 Storage Provider Abstraction.
- **Phase 8**: AI Creative Director Chat, Logo Intelligence, Design Critic, Brand Consistency Audit, Creative Briefs & Intelligence PDF Reports.
- **Phase 9**: Non-Destructive AI Image Editing Studio, Image Masks, Protected Logo Regions, Deterministic Logo Compositing, Upscale & Workflows.
- **Phase 10**: Market Research with Source Provenance, Competitor Comparison, Audience Segments & Personas, Brand Positioning, Naming/Tagline Candidates & 22-Section Strategy Reports (PDF/DOCX/JSON).
- **Phase 11**: AI Marketing & Brand Growth Studio (3-Mode Strategist, 6-Platform Social Studio, Copywriting Variants, Content Calendar, Ad Studio + Budget Estimator, 14-Stage Campaign ZIP, Sanitized Landing Pages, AES-256-GCM Platform Integrations, 7-Step Publishing Preflight, CSV Analytics & AI Performance Analyst).
- **Phase 12**: AI Brand Video & Motion Studio (12 Templates, Storyboard & Script Studio, Deterministic Logo Animation, Consent-Gated Voice Profiles & SRT Subtitles, 5-Track Timeline Editor, FFmpeg/Fallback MP4 & WebM Render + Editable Bundle, Video QC, System Diagnostics & SQLite Backup/Restore).
- **Overthinker Studio UI**: Olive-green header (`#394f29`), glass-pill navigation, solid black active pills (`#141812`), and `Plus Jakarta Sans` typography.
- **Release Packaging**: Windows x64 Local Edition package (`packaging/local-windows/`), Cloud Docker package (`packaging/cloud-docker/`), and GitHub Actions (`ci.yml`, `security.yml`, `release.yml`).

## [1.0.0-rc.1] - 2026-11-19
### Verified
- Release Candidate validation across all 12 phases (`17/17` API test suites passing, frontend TypeScript check passing, Local & Cloud packaging verified).
